How it works

1. Drop or book

Bring a bag to the counter, or ask us to collect it.

2. We tag it

We weigh or count, then note stains and “no dryer” items.

3. We wash

Lights and darks are separated. Delicates stay out of a hot cycle.

4. We line-dry

Anything that must not tumble is hung. The rest is dried as labelled.

5. We iron

Shirts, uniforms, and press orders go on the board, then on hangers.

6. We fold

Everyday wear, towels, and sheets go into a named bag.

7. Collect or we deliver

Pick up at the counter, or a dispatch rider brings the bag to you.

We do not take wedding gowns, leather, or carpets.

Those need a specialist. Ask us if you are unsure.
