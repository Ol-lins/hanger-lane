About

A Tesano laundry that sends clothes home on hangers.

Hanger Lane is a Tesano laundry. We wash for students, families, and small shops around campus.

In the shop

Every bag is tagged before it goes near a machine. Wash-and-fold orders are weighed in front of you. Pressed items leave on hangers, not crushed in a basin.

House rules

No surprise bleach

We call before using bleach or a strong stain treatment.

No unmarked bags

A name and number go on every basin before it enters the line.

No heat on delicates

If the label says do not tumble, we air-dry.
